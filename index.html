What we shipped

Three new capabilities

👁
At-a-glance channel column
An "Offered on" column with channel logos now appears on Items, Categories, Menus, and Modifier Groups — channel status visible without drilling in.
✏️
Bulk visibility edit modal
Select multiple entities, open "Edit channel visibility" from the bulk bar overflow, and toggle channels across the entire selection in a single modal.
🔍
Audit without opening items
Inspect which channels each entity is enabled or disabled on directly from the list view — no per-item navigation, no scrolling through individual records.

The change

Before & after

Before

Slow and error-prone at scale — especially across many items and channels

After

Bulk apply visibility changes across an entire selection in one flow
Operators self-serve what previously required a CS escalation
